# Authors

**Created and maintained by Sean Goodwin.**

GitHub contributor **faces, graphs, and commit counts are a commit counter, not a credit assignment.** They do not imply co-authorship, endorsement, or maintainer status. The authoritative graph is [Contributors](https://github.com/DealAppSeo/hyperdag-protocol/graphs/contributors). We do not reprint names here as a hall of fame.

If two git identities are the same person, fold them with a `.mailmap` at the repo root (none is required today). Do not invent a mailmap to hide history.

PRs: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Security: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
